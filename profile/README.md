Blume supplies municipal infrastructure systems to local authorities. Traffic
control, utility telemetry, transit coordination, camera aggregation, citizen
identity.

Each system is procured separately, deployed separately, and operated under a
separate contract. We are frequently asked whether these products form a single
platform. They do not.

Where our systems share a common event schema, identity provider, or transport
layer, this is a matter of engineering consistency and should not be read as
integration.

## Open source

Blume publishes reference implementations so that partners and integrators can
build against our interfaces without a commercial licence.

Releases are made available following internal review. Contributions are welcome
once a repository is public; all contributors are screened prior to merge.

## Data handling

Our retention periods, access controls, and audit procedures are set out in full
in the documentation accompanying each system.

We would encourage residents with concerns to read them. Most do not.

## Careers

We are hiring across engineering, data science, and operations. Blume offers
competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits, and a working environment in
which questions are answered promptly and appropriately.
